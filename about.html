Our Founding Journey

From an Office Gathering to a Nationally Recognized Production House

Evergreen Events was founded in 2011 by corporate event directors who realized that year-end office parties were consistently burdened by stressed HR managers, unpredictable vendors, and uninspired ballroom buffets.

We set out to build an event management firm built with corporate rigor: transparent line-item pricing, strict vendor safety standards, and bespoke thematic storytelling. Over the last 15 years, we have grown into India's premier holiday party and corporate gala planner, serving fast-growing startups and multinational enterprises alike.

“Our core philosophy has never wavered: when a company gathers to celebrate, every leader should be able to sit with their team, raise a glass, and celebrate without worrying about whether the caterer is on time.”

— Anita Rao, Founder & Managing Director

Our Methodology

Corporate Event Planning with Military Precision

Behind every magical festive evening lies a rigorous 400-point logistical run sheet. We manage venue insurance, municipal noise permits, power redundancies, VIP security details, and fire safety compliance so your leadership can rest easy.

  • ✓ Direct Hotel GM & Venue Sourcing: Preferential rates at Taj, Oberoi, Marriott, ITC and heritage properties.
  • ✓ Certified Production Rigging: Professional structural trusses, LED panels, and acoustic isolation systems.
  • ✓ White-Glove Guest Management: Digital QR badges, VIP protocols, and allergen-safe banquet catering.
