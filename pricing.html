Our Billing Ethics

How Our Cost Model Protects Your Company

Traditional event agencies conceal 20% to 35% markups on third-party catering and venue bills. Evergreen operates with open-book transparency.

1. Fixed Flat Planning Fee

You pay a single, guaranteed flat fee for complete production management, creative design, technical direction, and on-site crew coordination. No percentage escalations.

2. Zero Vendor Markups

Direct supplier contracts with hotels, caterers, and AV providers are billed at our corporate negotiated rates with 0% markup. Every single rupee of negotiated savings goes back to you.

3. Audit-Ready GST Line Items

Receive full vendor receipts, GST tax invoices, proof of permits, and detailed budget reconciliation accessible in your portal for immediate corporate finance compliance.
