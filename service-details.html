Our Workflow

Our 4-Phase Planning Blueprint

How we take your celebration from initial brainstorm to midnight confetti drop with zero operational friction.

Phase 1
Concept & Budget Discovery

We define your budget, survey team preferences, and propose 3 bespoke creative themes with mood boards and preliminary venue selections.

Phase 2
Venue Sourcing & Menu Tastings

We negotiate venue contracts with corporate rate locks, host menu tastings with 5-star executive chefs, and lock in headline entertainers.

Phase 3
Digital RSVP & Floor Choreography

Your customized client portal tracks live attendee confirmations, dietary requirements, table seating, and VIP transport logistics.

Phase 4
Day-Of White-Glove Execution

Our 15+ person production team executes sound checks, manages vendor deliveries, oversees stage cues, and handles post-event load-out.
