Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions corporate event organizers ask us.

For peak November and December dates (especially Thursday and Friday evenings), we strongly advise booking 8 to 12 weeks in advance to secure premium hotel ballrooms and prime talent. However, for fast-turnaround requests, our emergency production unit can produce boutique office celebrations in as little as 14 business days.

We operate with 100% transparency. Our planning fee is a clear, fixed flat rate. All direct vendor costs (venues, catering, audio-visual, decor) are billed at actual cost with zero hidden markups or commission kickbacks. You can inspect all itemized invoices directly in your client dashboard.

Yes, absolutely. All our catering partners are strictly FSSAI-certified. Our digital RSVP portal captures individual guest restrictions (Pure Vegetarian, Jain, Halal, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Nut Allergies). We deploy dedicated allergen-isolated live counters and color-coded table service on-site.

From high-definition LED video walls and intelligent moving-head lighting trusses to acoustic delay speakers, multi-mic wireless setups, and live broadcast audio mixing, we own and lease certified concert-grade gear engineered for clean acoustics and crisp speeches.

Every Evergreen event carries comprehensive ₹10 Crore Third-Party Public Liability insurance. We handle all required municipal sound permits, fire safety inspections, liquor licenses, and electrical safety certifications.
